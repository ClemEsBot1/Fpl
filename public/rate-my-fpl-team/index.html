FPL Checker checks your Fantasy Premier League team: how many points it is predicted to score, how it compares with the best squad £100m can buy, and what to change.

What the team check shows

  • Squad score: your predicted points against the best possible squad this gameweek.
  • Best XI and bench order, with the formation picked for you.
  • Captain: the best pick and a differential.
  • Flags: injured, suspended or doubtful players and those about to drop in price.
  • Chips: the best week for Bench Boost and Triple Captain with your squad.
  • Mini-leagues: where you are expected to finish, and your differentials against each rival.

Three ways to load your team

  1. Team ID: the number after /entry/ on your FPL Points page.
  2. Screenshot: upload a screenshot of your team from the FPL app; it is read on your device.
  3. Build one: pick a squad yourself to try out ideas before the season or a Wildcard.

Questions

Is my FPL team any good?

Compare its predicted points with the best squad for the same gameweek: FPL Checker shows this as a squad score, and which changes would close the gap.

Do I need to log in with my FPL account?

No. Your Team ID only shows FPL Checker what is already public; it can’t change your team.

Can it rate my team before the deadline?

Yes. Your picks for the coming gameweek are hidden by FPL until the deadline, so FPL Checker uses your latest visible team, which is the same unless you have made transfers.
