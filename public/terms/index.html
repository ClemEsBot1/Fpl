Terms and privacy

Plain-English terms for using FPL Checker, and what happens to your data. Last updated 16 October 2026.

1. About FPL Checker

FPL Checker (fplchecker.co.uk) is a free, independent tool for Fantasy Premier League managers. It is not run by, endorsed by or linked to the Premier League, Fantasy Premier League or any football club. Player and fixture data comes from FPL’s public data.

2. Using the site

  • By using FPL Checker you agree to these terms. If you don’t agree, please don’t use it.
  • Predictions, ratings and suggestions are estimates from statistical models. They can be wrong, and nothing on the site is a promise of points. Decisions about your FPL team are yours.
  • The site is provided as it is, free of charge, and may change, pause or stop at any time. As far as the law allows, we aren’t liable for any loss from using it, including lost FPL points, rank or prizes.
  • Don’t misuse the site: no attempts to break in, overload it, scrape it at scale, or use it to harass anyone.

3. Accounts

  • An account is optional. You need one only to save teams across devices or get emails.
  • Keep your password private. You’re responsible for what happens under your account.
  • If you add an email address, we send a code to confirm it belongs to you. It is used for password reset codes and, only if you turn them on, deadline emails.
  • We may suspend or delete accounts that break these terms.

4. Your data

  • What we store for an account: your username, a scrambled (hashed) password that nobody can read, your email if you add one and whether it’s confirmed, your saved teams, your deadline email setting, when you joined and when you agreed to these terms.
  • Without an account: nothing that identifies you. Your browser keeps a few settings on your device, such as the last Team ID you checked and that you agreed to these terms.
  • Cookies: one cookie that keeps you logged in. No advertising or tracking cookies. We use privacy-friendly visit counts from our host that don’t identify you.
  • Screenshots: a team screenshot you upload is read on your device. It is only sent to us if you choose to report a problem with it.
  • Who processes it: our hosting, database and email providers, only to run the site. We never sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • Push notifications, if you turn them on, use your browser’s push service, and you can turn them off any time.
  • Your rights: you can change or remove your email at any time, and delete your account and all its data yourself, straight away.

The browser extension’s privacy details are on the extension page.

5. Changes

We may update these terms. When we do, the date above changes and you’ll be asked to agree again the next time you start the site or create an account.

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.

Questions

Is FPL Checker linked to the Premier League?

No. It is an independent fan-made tool and not affiliated with the Premier League or Fantasy Premier League.

Does FPL Checker sell my data?

No. Account data is used only to run the site, never sold or shared for advertising.

How do I delete my account?

Log in, open the account menu and choose Delete account. It removes your account and everything stored with it straight away.
