How to find your FPL Team ID

Your FPL Team ID is the number FPL gives your team. It’s public and safe to share: it shows your team, but nobody can change anything with it.

Check my team

On the FPL website

  1. Go to fantasy.premierleague.com and log in.
  2. Click Points in the menu.
  3. Look at the address bar: it reads /entry/1234567/event/9. The number after /entry/ is your Team ID.

On the FPL app

  1. Open the app and go to Points, or open your team from a league table.
  2. Tap Share (or the three dots) and copy the link.
  3. Paste it anywhere: the number after /entry/ is your Team ID. If the app won’t show a link, log in on the website once instead.

What to do with it

  • Rate your team against the best possible squad.
  • Get your best captain and transfer suggestions.
  • See where you’re likely to finish in your mini-leagues.

Questions

Is it safe to share my FPL Team ID?

Yes. It only shows what anyone can already see on FPL: your team and points. It can’t be used to log in or make changes.

Is my Team ID the same every season?

No. FPL gives you a new Team ID each season when you pick a new team.

Can I find someone else’s Team ID?

Yes: open their team from a league table on the FPL website and read the number after /entry/ in the address.
