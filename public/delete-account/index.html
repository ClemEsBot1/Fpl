Delete your FPL Checker account

You can delete your FPL Checker account yourself at any time, in the Android app or on fplchecker.co.uk. It takes effect straight away.

Delete my account

How to delete it

  1. Open the FPL Checker app or fplchecker.co.uk and log in.
  2. Tap your username at the top to open your profile.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your password (if you signed up with Google and never set one, you won’t be asked), type DELETE and tap Delete my account.

The button above opens the site with the delete step ready; you’ll be asked to log in first if you aren’t.

What is deleted

  • Your account: username and password (stored only scrambled).
  • Your email address, and whether it was confirmed.
  • Your saved teams and squads, and your deadline email setting.
  • Any pending verification or password reset codes.

All of it is deleted immediately and permanently. It can’t be recovered, and your username and email become free for anyone to use again.

What is kept

  • Nothing tied to your account.
  • Push notifications are linked to your device, not your account: turn them off in the app or your browser settings to stop them.
  • A screenshot you chose to send with a problem report isn’t linked to your account.

Forgotten your password?

Use Forgot password? on the log-in screen to get a code by email, then delete the account. If your account has no email address and you can’t log in, it holds nothing that identifies you and can simply be left.

Questions

How long does deleting my FPL Checker account take?

It happens immediately once you confirm with your password and by typing DELETE.

Does deleting my FPL Checker account affect my Fantasy Premier League team?

No. FPL Checker is separate from Fantasy Premier League, so your FPL team and account are not touched.
