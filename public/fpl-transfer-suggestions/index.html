FPL transfer suggestions

Enter your FPL Team ID and FPL Checker suggests the transfers that add the most predicted points to your squad, within your bank and the three-per-club rule.

Open FPL Checker

How transfers are suggested

  • Over five gameweeks, not one: a player with an easy run ahead is worth more than one good fixture.
  • Hits counted: a -4 is only suggested when the points it gains over the next weeks are more than 4.
  • Rolling free transfers: sometimes the best move is none, and banking the transfer for next week.
  • Price changes: alerts when a player in your squad is about to drop in price, so you can sell first.

Plan it yourself

  1. Open your team and press Transfers.
  2. Remove a player and pick a replacement, or use Auto pick.
  3. See the predicted change over the next five gameweeks, after any hit, before you make it in FPL.

Questions

Should I take a -4 hit in FPL?

Only when the incoming player is predicted to outscore the outgoing one by more than 4 points over the next few gameweeks. FPL Checker shows that figure for every planned transfer.

Does FPL Checker make transfers for me?

No. It never logs into FPL; you make the transfers on the FPL site or app. It reads your public team from your Team ID.

Where do I find my FPL Team ID?

On the FPL website, open the Points page: your Team ID is the number after /entry/ in the address.
