FPL Checker Chrome extension

See FPL Checker’s predicted points without leaving the official Fantasy Premier League site. Free, no account, and it never changes your team.

Open FPL Checker

What it adds to the FPL site

  • Predicted points next to player names on Pick Team, Transfers, Points and the stats pages, for the coming gameweek.
  • A squad panel with your XI’s predicted score, captain counted twice, and each player’s fixture.
  • Your best captain, and how many points switching the armband would add.
  • Injury flags: a doubtful player’s badge turns amber, with FPL’s news on hover.
  • A toolbar popup with this gameweek’s top captains and a search for any player.

Get it

The extension is on its way to the Chrome Web Store. Until then, every prediction it shows is on this gameweek’s predictions page, or enter your Team ID on FPL Checker.

Privacy

  • The extension downloads FPL Checker’s predictions from fplchecker.co.uk. That request carries nothing about you or your team.
  • To show your squad it reads your team from the FPL site, in your browser, the same way the page itself does. Your team, Team ID and FPL login are never sent to FPL Checker or anyone else.
  • It stores only the predictions and your two settings, on your device. There are no analytics, ads or tracking, and nothing is sold or shared.
  • It can’t make transfers or change anything in your FPL account.

Questions

Is the FPL Checker extension free?

Yes, free with no account. It shows the same predictions as fplchecker.co.uk.

Does it need my FPL password?

No. It never asks for it and never sees it. If you are logged in to FPL, it reads your squad in your own browser to show its predicted points.

Does it work on the FPL app?

No, browser extensions only run on the FPL website in a desktop browser. On your phone, use fplchecker.co.uk.
